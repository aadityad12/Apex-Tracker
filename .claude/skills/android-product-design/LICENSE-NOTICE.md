# Derivation notice

`SKILL.md` in this directory is a derivative work of Anthropic's `frontend-design` skill,
published at https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md

Retained from the original: the framing of the design lead role, the "structure is
information" and "spend your boldness in one place" principles, the plan/critique/build/
critique process, the calibration list of AI-default looks, and the guidance on writing
in design.

Changed for this project: narrowed from general frontend to Android/Jetpack Compose;
added the locked ApexTracker identity (type, color, motion, spacing tokens); added the
Android platform requirements section (touch targets, dynamic type, insets, contrast,
accessibility, four states); added the explicit ban list; added the anti-generic
constraint section, which exists because this project's chosen palette overlaps two of
the AI-default looks the original skill calls out.

Refer to the original repository for the upstream license terms.
